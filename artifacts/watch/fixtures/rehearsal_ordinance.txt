ORDINANCE NO. R-2026-REHEARSAL-3

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING NOTICE BEFORE AUTOMATED TENANT SCREENING
IN THE CITY OF CAMBRIDGE

BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cambridge:

Section 8.100.010. Automated tenant screening notice.
Before using an automated tenant-screening system to evaluate an application for a residential rental unit in the City of Cambridge, a landlord or the landlord's agent shall give the applicant a written notice that identifies the system's provider and the categories of information the system will consider.

Section 8.100.020. Coverage.
This chapter applies to every application for a residential rental unit located in the City of Cambridge.

Section 8.100.030. Effective date.
This ordinance takes effect on January 20, 2027.

Enacted December 5, 2026.

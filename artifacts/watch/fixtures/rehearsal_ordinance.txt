ORDINANCE NO. R-2026-REHEARSAL

AN ORDINANCE LIMITING RENTAL APPLICATION FEES IN THE CITY OF CAMBRIDGE

BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of Cambridge:

Section 8.99.010. Application fees.
No landlord or agent of a landlord shall charge a prospective tenant an application fee or screening fee that exceeds fifty dollars ($50) for any rental unit in a building with five or more units.

Section 8.99.020. Exemptions.
This chapter does not apply to a building with fewer than five units in which the owner lives.

Section 8.99.030. Effective date.
This ordinance shall take effect on the first day of the second month next following the date of enactment.
